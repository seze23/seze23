## 🧠 What I work on

**Estimation under partial observation.** Something has to build a usable estimate of a world it cannot see directly, and then commit to acting on it.

* [**MotorMetamersuPNC**](https://github.com/seze23/MotorMetamersuPNC) - Proprioceptive world models. A 25-muscle OpenSim musculoskeletal simulation feeding synthetic spindle signals to pretrained CNNs, testing whether they can reconstruct arm position across eight reach directions from proprioception alone. (WIP) Task-optimized networks build internal models of a body nobody specified. *NIH uPNC, Carnegie Mellon Neuroscience Institute.*
* [**Relay**](https://github.com/seze23/YC-Medplum-Hack) - Autonomous patient operations for outpatient PT clinics, built in 7 hours at Medplum x YC. Answers a real phone line, resolves the caller during the ring, verifies insurance over X12, books a slot, and writes eight FHIR resources. 
* [**Segment-and-Track-Anything**](https://github.com/seze23/Segment-and-Track-Anything-Project) - Multimodal computer-vision benchmark evaluating SAM, GroundingDINO, and Lightning Pose on behavioral video. *Paninski Lab, Columbia Zuckerman Institute.*
* [**CA3-Model**](https://github.com/seze23/CA3-Model) - Emergence of seizure activity from rhythmic traces in a model hippocampal CA3 circuit; populations of atypical PYR, PV, and OLM cells probing mechanisms of hyperexcitability in ASD. My first (non-gaming or hacking related) coding project built at 16 *Dura-Bernal & Alarcon Labs, SUNY Downstate.*

## ☑ How I build

Every system I write is a claim about the world, and I'd like to be able to check it.

* **Separate estimation from action.** The part that *guesses* and the part that *acts* should not be the same object.
* **Determinism where the stakes are.** 
* **Name your reward function.** 
* **Capture everything the first time it works.** Every external response gets written to a fixture. Sandboxes fall over, APIs rate-limit, and the run you can't reproduce is a run you didn't do. This is what makes late architectural changes survivable.
* **The failure distribution is the result.** An accuracy number tells you how well a system did. Its errors tell you what it assumed. I've never learned more from a mean than from the shape around it.
